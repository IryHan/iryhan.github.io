01 / About关于我

Teaching is where ideas meet people.

教学，是思想与真实的人相遇的地方。

I am an undergraduate student at The Education University of Hong Kong, building a foundation in English language education while exploring TESOL and applied linguistics.

我目前就读于香港教育大学，在学习英语语言教育的同时，持续探索 TESOL、应用语言学，以及研究如何回应真实课堂中的问题。

My interests sit at the intersection of language learning, classroom practice, and educational research. I am especially curious about language assessment, learner experience, and how teachers can turn linguistic knowledge into meaningful learning opportunities.

我的兴趣位于语言学习、课堂实践与教育研究的交汇处。我尤其关注语言评估、学习者体验，以及教师如何将语言学知识转化为有意义的学习机会。

Alongside academic study, I value teaching assistance, service learning, peer support, and reflective practice. I see each classroom as a place to learn—not only for students, but for teachers too.

在学术学习之外，我也重视助教、服务学习、朋辈支持与教学反思。我相信课堂不仅是学生学习的地方，也是教师持续学习的地方。

“Good teaching begins with curiosity about the learner—and the humility to keep learning.”
「好的教学，始于对学习者的好奇，也始于教师持续学习的谦逊。」
